Privacy Policy

The short version: this is a concept demo website. It collects nothing, tracks nothing, and sets no cookies of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no account system, and no server-side processing on this demo. The contact form on this concept site does not send your message anywhere — it only shows an on-screen notice.

Third parties that load

Two outside services load when you visit, and they may see your visit the way any website visit is visible to its host:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — delivers the site's typefaces.
  • Google Maps (maps.google.com) — only on the Find Us page, inside the map frame, to show the location.

We chose not to add analytics, social-media pixels, or any other tracker. The live site for the real business would get its own policy written for whatever it actually uses.
